Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

WoW Train (glosx.app) is developed by GLOSX (Sebastián Jasinsky). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. The short version: we don't require an account, we don't process payments, and we never sell your data.

1. Analytics & Tracking

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand site traffic — pages visited, approximate location by country, device type and referral source. We also use Google Ads and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) conversion tracking to measure whether our advertising is working. None of this identifies you personally; it's aggregate usage data.

2. Cookies

The site uses cookies from Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta for the analytics and advertising purposes described above. See our Cookie Policy for details on what's stored and how to opt out.

3. Payments & Bookings

WoW Train never processes payments. Every booking — trains via Klook, and other travel essentials via our partners — is completed on that partner's own official, secure platform. WoW Train never receives, processes, or stores any payment card data.

4. Affiliate Links

WoW Train participates in affiliate programs (including Klook, Trainline, Kiwitaxi, Yesim, Tiqets, Radical Storage and EconomyBookings). Clicking a booking link may set a tracking cookie so the partner can attribute the booking to us — this is how we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. WoW Train does not receive any personal or payment data from this process; it stays with the partner, subject to their own privacy policy.

5. Train Schedule Data

Route and schedule information shown on the site comes from public timetable data and official railway sources. This browsing activity is not linked to your identity.

6. Children's Privacy

The site does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13 and contains no features directed at children.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
