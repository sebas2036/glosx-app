How long is the train from Brussels to Bruges?

Journey time is about 0h 50m on the quickest service. Departures run multiple times daily, so there's usually a slot that fits your plans.

Which trains run from Brussels to Bruges?

SNCB operates this route. A single search across the day's trains makes it easy to line up a departure with the fare you want.

Worth knowing

Bruges' entire medieval centre — canals, guild houses and belfry included — is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and it's reachable from a European capital in under an hour by train, which is unusually fast for a city this historically intact. Most visitors do it as a day trip rather than an overnight stay.

Stations: where you leave and where you arrive

  • Brussels Midi, Central or Nord — the intercity trains to Bruges call at all three main Brussels stations, so choose the one nearest your hotel.
  • Bruges station is about 1.5 km from the Markt, roughly a 20–25 minute walk, with buses and taxis outside.

Brussels to Bruges train times and frequency

Intercity trains take roughly 50 minutes to an hour and run about every half hour through the day, so you can turn up and go without planning around one departure. A few services are slower stopping trains, so check the journey time shown for the train you choose.

Best time to travel

Bruges is crowded in summer and on weekends, especially between late morning and mid-afternoon. Arriving early in the morning or staying until evening is the best way to enjoy the canals with fewer people.

What to do when you arrive in Bruges

Bruges is compact and best explored on foot. Highlights:

  • The Markt and the Belfry — the medieval market square and its 13th-century bell tower.
  • The canals and the Beguinage — peaceful corners away from the main streets.
  • Burg square and the Basilica of the Holy Blood — the old civic centre.
  • Boat tour or a walk along the canals — a classic way to see the old town in an hour.

Other ways to get from Brussels to Bruges

  • Coach — services exist but take longer than the train.
  • Car — about an hour, but parking in Bruges is limited and expensive; park outside the centre.
