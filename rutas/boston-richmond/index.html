Your trip guide

What is worth knowing for the way you travel, from official sources.

First time (or a nervous traveler)

  • Get to the station about 30 minutes early; 60 if you are checking bags or need assistance.
  • The track or boarding gate shows on the departure screens about 15 minutes before departure. At some stations the gate closes 3 to 10 minutes before.
  • On board, the conductor scans your ticket: you can keep it on your phone.
  • If the train reaches your station late, it may stop for less time than the timetable shows: listen for the announcement.

Frequent traveler

  • The fastest direct train takes 10 h 37 min; there are 3 a day in this direction, the first at 6:10 am.
  • Northeast Regional trains have a Quiet Car: no phone calls and lower lighting.

Still deciding

  • Fares usually rise as the date gets closer, so booking ahead tends to cost less.
  • If you don't cancel before departure and don't travel, the ticket is forfeited. Change and refund rules depend on the fare: check them before you pay.

Before you travel: what to check

  • ID: passengers 18 and over may be asked for a valid government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license or similar). It is required if you buy a ticket on board or check bags, and it must not be expired.
  • Amtrak carry-on: up to 2 bags of 50 lbs and 28 × 22 × 14 inches each, plus personal items that fit under the seat. Check how many personal items your train allows when you book.
  • Free Wi-Fi on most Amtrak trains.
  • Food: you can bring your own food and drinks to have at your seat. Anything bought in the dining or café car is eaten there. On a long trip, pack some extra food in case of delays.
  • Children: ages 2 to 12 pay half when traveling with an adult (one child per adult) and cannot travel alone. Under 2s ride free on a lap.
  • Pets: Amtrak only takes dogs and cats on trips of up to 7 hours, so they cannot travel on this full route. Service animals are welcome on all trains.

Safety

  • Keep your bags in sight and take them with you when you move around the train.
  • To report something suspicious or ask for help: Amtrak Police 1-800-331-0008, text 27311, or 911. You can also speak to any uniformed officer or crew member.
  • Save those numbers on your phone before you leave.

Good to know

  • In the US, weight is in pounds, distance in miles and temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.
  • The conductor usually announces each stop over the speakers.
