Beijing → Shenyang

By WoW Train · Updated November 2026 · 3 min read

The fastest direct train from Beijing to Shenyang takes about 2 hours 29 minutes. Shenyang, the first capital of the Qing dynasty, has its own imperial palace, smaller than Beijing’s Forbidden City.

Key facts

  • Fastest journey: about 2h29.
  • Direct trains on the day we checked (27 November 2026): 64.
  • First and last departure that day: 05:54 and 20:43.
  • Main stations: Beijing Chaoyang → Shenyang North.

Which train to take

On the day we checked, Klook listed 64 direct trains between Beijing and Shenyang. The fastest takes about 2h29; others stop more often and take longer, so compare journey times before you book. There are fewer trains on this route, so book a few days ahead in busy periods such as Chinese New Year and the October Golden Week.

Tips before you go

  • Most fast trains leave from Beijing Chaoyang, not Beijing South, and arrive at Shenyang North.
  • Train tickets in mainland China are issued in the passenger’s name: you need your passport to book and to board.
  • Allow 30 to 45 minutes before departure for the security check and the ticket and ID check at the station.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the train from Beijing to Shenyang?

The fastest direct train takes about 2 hours 29 minutes. Journey times vary from train to train, so check the one you book.

How many trains run between Beijing and Shenyang?

On the day we checked (27 November 2026), Klook listed 64 direct trains, the first at 05:54 and the last at 20:43.

Do I need my passport to take the train in China?

Yes. Tickets are issued in the passenger’s name and are checked against your passport at the station.
