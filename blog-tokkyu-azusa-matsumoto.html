Limited Express Azusa to Matsumoto: How to Ride It from Shinjuku and Tokyo

By WoW Train · November 2026 · 5 min read

Matsumoto, with its black castle and the Northern Alps behind it, is one of the easiest side trips from Tokyo: the Azusa limited express gets you there with no change of train. This guide covers journey times by departure station, how seating works now that the train is fully reserved, and the best option if you are coming from Nagoya or Nagano instead.

Journey time depends on where you board

On the fastest trains, Shinjuku to Matsumoto takes about 2h29 and Tokyo Station to Matsumoto about 2h55, both direct. Only a few Azusa trains start at Tokyo Station, so if you are staying in central Tokyo it is usually easier to plan around Shinjuku.

  • Shinjuku → Matsumoto: about 2h29 on the Azusa
  • Tokyo → Matsumoto: about 2h55 on the Azusa

Every seat is reserved, and the "seat-unspecified" ticket

Since 16 March 2019 the ordinary cars of the Azusa (and its sister train, the Kaiji) are fully reserved: there are no non-reserved cars. If you would rather not pick a seat, you can buy a seat-unspecified ticket and sit in any free seat; a red lamp above the seat shows it has not been sold. You are not guaranteed a seat that way, so on weekends and Japanese holidays it is safer to reserve one in advance.

From Nagoya or Nagano, take the Shinano

If you are travelling from central Japan, the Shinano limited express runs direct to Matsumoto:

  • Nagoya → Matsumoto: about 2h03 on the Shinano
  • Nagano → Matsumoto: about 50 minutes on the Shinano

From Tokyo you could also take the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Nagano and change to the Shinano, but that adds a change of train. The Azusa's big advantage is that it goes all the way without one.

Before you go

  • Matsumoto Castle is within walking distance of the station; coin lockers at the station let you explore without your bags.
  • If you are heading on to Kamikochi or Hakuba, leave some margin for the connection in Matsumoto.
  • Times and seat availability change day to day: check the latest times for your date on each route page.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Azusa from Shinjuku to Matsumoto?

About 2 hours 29 minutes on the fastest trains, with no change.

Can I go from Tokyo Station to Matsumoto without changing trains?

Yes. Azusa trains that start at Tokyo Station reach Matsumoto in about 2 hours 55 minutes, but there are only a few of them.

Does the Azusa have non-reserved seats?

No. Since 16 March 2019 all ordinary cars are reserved; if you do not want to pick a seat you can use a seat-unspecified ticket and take any free seat.

What is the fastest train from Nagoya to Matsumoto?

The Shinano limited express, about 2 hours 3 minutes with no change.
