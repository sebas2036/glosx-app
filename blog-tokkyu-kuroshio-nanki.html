Kuroshio Limited Express: Osaka to Wakayama, Kii-Tanabe and Shirahama

By WoW Train · November 2026 · 5 min read

Wakayama Castle, Kii-Tanabe at the start of the Kumano Kodo pilgrimage trails, and the beaches and hot springs of Shirahama: the Kuroshio limited express links them all to Osaka and Kyoto along the west coast of the Kii Peninsula. Since 18 March 2023 it also stops at Osaka Station, so you can board in Umeda with no change.

Where to board at Osaka Station

The Kuroshio uses the Umekita underground platforms at Osaka Station. It used to pass through without stopping; it has stopped there since the timetable change of 18 March 2023. These platforms are some distance from the main above-ground platforms, so allow a few extra minutes to reach them.

Journey times by destination

All of these are direct (fastest trains):

  • Osaka → Wakayama: about 1h02 (castle town)
  • Osaka → Kii-Tanabe: about 2h16 (gateway to the Kumano Kodo)
  • Osaka → Shirahama: about 2h28 (seaside hot-spring resort)

From Kyoto

Some Kuroshio trains start at Kyoto Station, so you can reach Wakayama without changing:

  • Kyoto → Wakayama: about 1h36

Before you go

  • Walking the Kumano Kodo? Pick your train so it connects with the bus from Kii-Tanabe.
  • Shirahama gets busy at weekends and in the summer holidays; reserve seats early.
  • Times and seat availability change day to day: check the latest times for your date on each route page.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Kuroshio stop at Osaka Station?

Yes. Since 18 March 2023 it stops at the Umekita underground platforms of Osaka Station.

How long is the Kuroshio from Osaka to Wakayama?

About 1 hour 2 minutes on the fastest trains, with no change.

Can I go from Osaka to Shirahama without changing trains?

Yes. The Kuroshio reaches Shirahama in about 2 hours 28 minutes, direct.

Which station is closest to the Kumano Kodo?

Kii-Tanabe, the gateway to the Nakahechi route, about 2 hours 16 minutes from Osaka on the Kuroshio.
