How it was calculated

  • Source: the official GTFS timetables published by SBB (opentransportdata.swiss), ÖBB, Trafiklab / Samtrafiken, Digitraffic / Fintraffic, Entur and Renfe.
  • Only direct trains count; coupled trains running at the same times count once.
  • Reference days: one weekday, one Saturday and one Sunday of the autumn 2026 timetable.
  • Timetables change: check the operator before you travel.
