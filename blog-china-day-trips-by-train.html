China Day Trips by Train: 15 Easy Escapes from Shanghai, Beijing and More

By WoW Train · November 2026 · 6 min read

China’s high-speed trains put gardens, water towns, sacred mountains and ancient capitals within easy reach of the big cities. These are the best day trips by train, each with the fastest journey time and the number of trains we found on Klook.

From Shanghai

  • Shanghai → Suzhou · from 21 min · 622 trains on the day we checked. Suzhou, known for its classical gardens and canals, is so close that many visitors go for the day.
  • Shanghai → Hangzhou · from 45 min · 357 trains on the day we checked. Hangzhou, home of West Lake, is one of the most popular trips from Shanghai and easy to do as a day trip.
  • Shanghai → Wuxi · from 25 min · 290 trains on the day we checked. Wuxi, on the shores of Lake Tai and the Grand Canal, is close enough to Shanghai for a day trip.
  • Shanghai → Nanjing · from 59 min · 334 trains on the day we checked. Nanjing, a former capital of China on the Yangtze, is one of the most frequent connections from Shanghai.
  • Hangzhou → Shaoxing · from 18 min · 64 trains on the day we checked. Shaoxing, a canal town known for its rice wine and as the hometown of the writer Lu Xun, is a very short hop from Hangzhou.

From Beijing

  • Beijing → Tianjin · from 30 min · 319 trains on the day we checked. Tianjin, a port city with old European-style quarters, is close enough to Beijing for a day trip.
  • Beijing → Jinan · from 1h22 · 136 trains on the day we checked. Jinan, the “city of springs”, is the first big stop on the Beijing–Shanghai line and the gateway to Mount Tai.
  • Beijing → Tai'an · from 1h34 · 22 trains on the day we checked. Tai’an sits at the foot of Mount Tai, the most revered of China’s five sacred mountains.
  • Beijing → Datong · from 1h45 · 57 trains on the day we checked. Datong is the base for the Yungang Grottoes, with thousands of Buddhist statues carved into the cliffs.

From Xi’an, Chengdu, Guangzhou and Zhengzhou

  • Xi'an → Luoyang · from 1h15 · 116 trains on the day we checked. Luoyang, home of the Longmen Grottoes, is a little over an hour east of Xi’an.
  • Chengdu → Leshan · from 41 min · 66 trains on the day we checked. Leshan, home of the Giant Buddha carved into a riverside cliff, is under an hour from Chengdu on the fastest trains.
  • Chengdu → Chongqing · from 1h06 · 145 trains on the day we checked. Two great cities of southwest China, both famous for spicy Sichuan-style food, about an hour apart.
  • Guangzhou → Shenzhen · from 29 min · 518 trains on the day we checked. The two great cities of the Pearl River Delta are linked by trains throughout the day.
  • Guangzhou → Zhuhai · from 44 min · 140 trains on the day we checked. Zhuhai, on the coast next to Macau, is less than an hour from Guangzhou.
  • Zhengzhou → Kaifeng · from 17 min · 249 trains on the day we checked. Kaifeng, a former imperial capital of the Song dynasty, is a very short hop from Zhengzhou.

Journey times and trains per day: Klook search for 27 November 2026. Times change with the day and the train, so check the live schedule when you book.

Tips for a day trip by train

  • Book the return train at the same time as the outward one, especially on weekends.
  • Big cities have several stations: check the one on each ticket, and allow time to cross the city.
  • Arrive 30 to 45 minutes before departure for the security check, and carry your passport: tickets are in your name.
  • Leave yourself a margin on the way back: if you miss your train, the next one may be full.
